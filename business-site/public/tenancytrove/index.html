Private by design

Your records stay with you

TenancyTrove has no account or automatic evidence upload. Rental records and selected file bytes live on your device or in your browser profile. Choose when to export a backup, share a pack or email support. iPhone text capture uses on-device Apple Vision; web text capture uses hosted OCR assets without sending your image or recognised words to an OCR server.

Keep a separate backup. The local database and ZIP/PDF exports are not encrypted. Clearing browser storage or removing the app can remove records that you have not backed up. TenancyTrove does not replace an official condition report, lodge a form or provide legal advice.
