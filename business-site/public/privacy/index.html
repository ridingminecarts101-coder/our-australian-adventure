Privacy at RL Applications

How the studio website and general enquiries handle information, with separate details for each current app.

Last updated 14 September 2026

Who operates this website

This website and RL Applications are operated by Riley Nicholas Lawler, a sole trader in Australia. The business ABN is 92 363 169 656. Privacy questions can be sent to rambodog555@gmail.com.

What this page covers

This page covers visits to rlapplications.com and general messages sent to RL Applications. Each app can handle different information for different purposes, so its own privacy page supplements this studio page. The current app supplement is Wayfinder privacy.

Website visits

The website is a static site hosted by Cloudflare Pages. It has no account sign-in, forms, advertising, analytics script or advertising cookies. Cloudflare may receive normal connection and security information, such as an IP address, browser details, requested pages and request times, when it delivers and protects the site. Cloudflare handles that information under its own infrastructure and terms.

Emails and support

If you email RL Applications, the studio receives the address you use, your message and any attachments or other details you choose to provide. That information is used to understand and answer the enquiry, investigate faults or misuse, keep a proportionate support history and meet legal obligations. Google’s email infrastructure carries and stores messages sent to the current public address. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, app-store passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Support information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. A specific period depends on the enquiry, any ongoing issue and applicable record-keeping obligations. RL Applications does not sell or rent support messages or use them for advertising.

Service providers and overseas handling

Cloudflare provides website delivery and security, and Google provides the current support email service. Their infrastructure may process information outside Australia. Wayfinder separately uses Resend to deliver account confirmation and recovery messages; that account-email processing, including its configured Tokyo sending region and limits, is described in the Wayfinder privacy page. Resend does not receive Wayfinder photos through that account-email flow, and its no-reply sender is not the studio support mailbox. RL Applications may also disclose relevant information where reasonably needed to obtain professional advice, respond to a lawful request or protect the studio and its users. App providers and app data are described in the relevant app privacy page.

Access, correction and questions

You can ask what personal information RL Applications holds about your enquiry, request a correction, or raise a privacy concern by emailing the address above. Enough information may be requested to locate the relevant messages and verify that they relate to you. RL Applications will review the concern and respond with the outcome or any further information reasonably needed. An app account deletion request should use the app-specific deletion route.
