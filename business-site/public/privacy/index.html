Privacy at RL Applications

How the studio website and general enquiries handle information, with separate details for each current app.

Last updated 15 September 2026

Who operates this website

This website and RL Applications are operated by Riley Lawler, a sole trader in Australia. The business ABN is 92 363 169 656. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to help.rlapplications@gmail.com.

What this page covers

This page covers visits to rlapplications.com and general messages sent to RL Applications. Each app can handle different information for different purposes, so its own privacy page supplements this studio page. The current app supplement is Wayfinder privacy.

Website visits

The website is a static site hosted by Cloudflare Pages. It has no account sign-in, forms, advertising, analytics script or advertising cookies. Cloudflare may receive normal connection and security information, such as an IP address, browser details, requested pages and request times, when it delivers and protects the site. Cloudflare handles that information under its own infrastructure and terms.

Emails and support

RL Applications previously published a personal Gmail address for support. If you used that address, the studio received the address you used, your message and any attachments or other details you chose to provide. That information may remain in Google’s email infrastructure and a proportionate support history while needed to understand and answer the enquiry, investigate faults or misuse, or meet legal obligations. Removing the address from this website does not erase messages already received.

The current monitored public mailbox is help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details, app-store passwords or unnecessary identity documents.

Wayfinder may add an optional, separately consented, text-only Community recommendation review route. If enabled, a chosen public display name, recommendation text and source link can be emailed to this mailbox through Resend and processed by a connected OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT task. It does not send a Wayfinder sign-in email or Memories photo in that task email. The Wayfinder privacy supplement explains the exact choice, provider copies, private denial reason and appeal route. General support messages are not automatically Community approvals.

Support information is kept only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. A specific period depends on the enquiry, any ongoing issue and applicable record-keeping obligations. RL Applications does not sell or rent support messages or use them for advertising.

Service providers and overseas handling

Cloudflare provides website delivery and security. Google provides the current Gmail support service and may continue to store historical messages sent to the previous public address. Their infrastructure may process information outside Australia. Wayfinder separately uses Resend to deliver account confirmation and recovery messages; that account-email processing, including its configured Tokyo sending region and limits, is described in the Wayfinder privacy page. If the separate consented Community review route is enabled, Resend also sends limited text-only task email to Gmail, and OpenAI processes its contents through the connected Codex/ChatGPT account; provider retention differs and is detailed on the app page. Resend does not receive Wayfinder photos through either email flow, and its no-reply sender is not a studio support mailbox. RL Applications may also disclose relevant information where reasonably needed to obtain professional advice, respond to a lawful request or protect the studio and its users. App providers and app data are described in the relevant app privacy page.

Access, correction and questions

You can email help.rlapplications@gmail.com to ask what personal information RL Applications holds about an enquiry, request a correction or raise a privacy concern. Enough information may be requested to locate relevant messages and verify that they relate to you. App account controls and their current limits are described on the app-specific deletion route.

App-specific information

Wayfinder

Wayfinder has separate information about accounts, synced progress, device-local photos and optional user-controlled encrypted photo export/import, a retained historical backup, trips, location lookup, groups, Community features, service providers and the native iOS purchase setup. Photo backup files remain with the destination the user chooses; the studio does not automatically receive them.
