Privacy information

Your data in Wayfinder

A plain-language account of what the testing build may handle, why it is used and what remains under verification.

Last updated 14 September 2026

Testing status: Email verification and password recovery are available. Group sharing is currently unavailable, Community protections are still being tested, and purchases are not active in the web review build. Contact support for help with account deletion. Avoid posting sensitive material and keep another copy of important photos and trip notes during testing.

Information Wayfinder may handle

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, sign-in and verification information, and an optional display name. Supabase handles account authentication and passwords.
  • Personal activity: completed-adventure ticks and dates, shortlist choices, ratings and notes.
  • Trips: trip names, dates, selected adventure identifiers and trip notes.
  • Photos: a photo you select, a resized device-local copy and related adventure or date metadata. Older photos already held in Wayfinder's private cloud storage remain readable.
  • Sharing: group membership, sharing choices and records previously shared with a group.
  • Community activity: submissions, votes, star ratings, reports and blocks if those features are used when available.
  • Support: the email address, message and attachments you choose to send.
  • Device data: session data, preferences, cached app files, local browser storage, IndexedDB records and queues used to support offline work and later syncing.

Location and network information

If you choose Near me and allow location access, your coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud to identify your region. That provider also receives your IP address and describes using observations from its free service to improve IP geolocation. Wayfinder does not save these coordinates in your account or track your route. You can browse destinations without location permission. How BigDataCloud uses this request.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, sync personal progress, show saved trips and device-local or older hosted photos, apply sharing choices, support optional Community functions, respond to support requests, investigate abuse or faults and protect the service. Wayfinder does not sell or rent your personal data and does not include advertising or an advertising analytics SDK.

Photos added in current versions

When you add a new photo in the current device-local build, Wayfinder resizes it and keeps the resulting copy only in that app or browser on the device. In the native app it is stored in Wayfinder's private app files, not copied to the phone's photo gallery. In the PWA it is stored in the browser's IndexedDB storage. This build does not upload a new photo image or its related photo record to the RL Applications backend or GitHub. New photos do not sync with account progress and will not appear when the same account signs in on another device. Until the matching server boundary is deployed, an older client may still use Wayfinder's former cloud-upload path.

Signing out keeps these device-local photos so they are available if the same account signs in again; Wayfinder filters them by account while another account is signed in. Removing the native app, clearing its app data, or clearing the PWA's browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Wayfinder does not currently provide a photo export or device-backup feature, so keep an independent copy of any important photo. Native releases are configured to exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data, including new photos, from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. The PWA cannot control copies a browser, operating system or user-configured device backup may retain outside its site storage.

Photos uploaded by an earlier version remain read-only in Wayfinder's private cloud storage and may still be shown in the app. They are handled under the older sharing and deletion rules below.

Groups and older shared records

The intended completion-sharing control covers completed ticks, completion dates and the display name used in the group. It does not automatically share personal notes, ratings, shortlists, new photos or trips. Those categories need their own explicit sharing action where supported.

Older photos or trips that a person explicitly shared may remain available to the group until that share is removed or the person leaves, subject to the final verified behaviour. Turning off completion sharing does not withdraw older records. Another person may also retain a copy of information they previously saw or saved. Group controls currently show maintenance while ownership protections are prepared.

Community recommendations are intended for other app users to read, together with your display name. Votes, ratings, reports and block choices are also stored to operate those features. Community protections are still being tested; do not post private or sensitive information there.

Service providers and hosting

  • Supabase provides authentication, database infrastructure and private storage for older cloud photos.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the current Wayfinder PWA.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts this support and policy website. These hosting providers may receive normal connection information such as IP address and browser details.
  • BigDataCloud receives coordinates when you choose the optional Near me lookup.
  • Resend sends Wayfinder account confirmation and recovery email for Supabase. It receives the recipient email address, the authentication message and link or code, and delivery metadata needed to send and troubleshoot the message. The configured sending region is Tokyo (ap-northeast-1), but that setting does not mean all processing or storage stays in Japan. Open and click tracking are disabled. Resend does not receive Wayfinder photos through this account-email flow. Resend privacy policy.
  • Google email infrastructure carries messages sent to the current public support address. The Resend no-reply address is only an automated account sender and is not a monitored support mailbox.

Providers may process information outside Australia under their own infrastructure and terms. This website contains no advertising or analytics script and does not set advertising cookies.

Future purchases

Purchases are not active in the PWA review build. If purchases are enabled in future native releases, Apple or Google will process payment and may keep transaction records. RevenueCat is planned to receive an app-user identifier, purchase receipt information and entitlement status so access can be restored. RL Applications does not plan to receive or store full payment-card numbers.

Optional reminders

Adventure reminders are off unless you enable them. They are generated on your device using your reminder settings.

Storage, security and retention

Wayfinder uses device storage for new photos, offline access and queued changes, and hosted services for accounts, progress syncing and older cloud photos. No system can promise perfect security. Account, group, Community and deletion controls are being tested before broader release.

Hosted information is kept while needed to provide the service, resolve support or security issues, meet legal obligations and maintain proportionate provider backups. Precise production retention periods have not yet been finalised. Removing the app or clearing one browser can remove new device-local photos but does not itself delete hosted account data or older cloud photos.

Your choices

  • Decline location permission and browse without Near me.
  • Choose whether to add a device-local photo or use Community and group features when available.
  • Review or change personal content through the app where the relevant feature is operating.
  • Ask a privacy question, request access or correction, or request account deletion by email.

Children

Wayfinder is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied personal information should contact support.

Policy changes

This page may change as testing finishes, services are configured and native versions are prepared. Material changes will be reflected here with a new update date.
