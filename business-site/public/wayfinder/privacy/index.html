Privacy information

Your data in Wayfinder

What Wayfinder handles, how sharing works and what remains separate from the release build.

Last updated 15 September 2026

Release status: A verified account is required to use Wayfinder. Email verification, password recovery, optional group sharing and in-app account deletion are available. New and edited Community recommendations stay private until reviewed and may take time to appear to others; reporting and blocking are available. The web review build cannot sell packs and the App Store listing is not live. Keep another copy of important photos and trip notes.

Who operates Wayfinder

Wayfinder is developed and operated by Riley Lawler, a sole trader operating RL Applications in Australia (ABN 92 363 169 656). Questions and privacy requests can be emailed to help.rlapplications@gmail.com. The studio privacy page explains how the RL Applications website and historical general enquiries are handled.

Information Wayfinder may handle

  • Account information: email address, account identifier, sign-in and verification information, and an optional display name. Supabase handles account authentication and passwords.
  • Personal activity: completed-adventure ticks and dates, shortlist choices, ratings and notes.
  • Trips: trip names, dates, selected adventure identifiers and trip notes.
  • Photos: a photo you select, a resized device-local copy and related adventure or date metadata kept on that device.
  • Sharing: group membership, sharing choices and records previously shared with a group.
  • Community activity: submissions, votes, star ratings, reports and blocks if those features are used when available.
  • Support: the email address, message and attachments you choose to send.
  • Device data: session data, preferences, cached app files, local browser storage, IndexedDB records and queues used to support offline work and later syncing.

Location and network information

Near me asks for your choice before each lookup. If you choose to proceed and allow location access, Wayfinder sends your coordinates to BigDataCloud to identify your region. That provider also receives your IP address and describes using observations from its free service to improve IP geolocation. Wayfinder does not save these coordinates in your account, link the lookup to your account, or track your route. The provider may process the request under its own terms; Wayfinder does not control its retention. You can browse destinations without Near me or location permission. How BigDataCloud uses this request.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, sync personal progress, show saved trips and device-local photos, apply sharing choices, support optional Community functions, respond to support requests, investigate abuse or faults and protect the service. Wayfinder does not sell or rent your personal data and does not include advertising or an advertising analytics SDK.

Photos added in current versions

When you add a new photo, Wayfinder resizes it and keeps the resulting copy only in that app or browser on the device. In the native app it is stored in Wayfinder's private app files, not copied to the phone's photo gallery. In the PWA it is stored in the browser's IndexedDB storage. Current clients cannot upload a new photo image or its related photo record to the RL Applications backend or GitHub. New photos do not sync with account progress and will not appear when the same account signs in on another device.

Signing out keeps these device-local photos so they are available if the same account signs in again; Wayfinder filters them by account while another account is signed in. Removing the native app, clearing its app data, or clearing the PWA's browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Under Me → Settings, you can manually export your local photos as password-encrypted, numbered .wfpbackup files and import them on another device signed in to the same Wayfinder account. Save every part. Wayfinder does not upload a backup to RL Applications or automatically sync photos. You choose where to keep or transfer the files; Files, AirDrop, email or a cloud-storage provider may hold a separate copy under their own terms. RL Applications cannot recover a file if you forget its backup password. Native releases are configured to exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data, including new photos, from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. The PWA cannot control copies a browser, operating system or user-configured device backup may retain outside its site storage.

On 15 September 2026, the 15 historical photo objects and all 11 active photo records were removed from Wayfinder's production cloud service. RL Applications still retains a separate Desktop backup of those historical originals and related database and storage snapshots for migration and rollback evidence. That backup is not available through the app and remains subject to a final retention decision. This means RL Applications must not claim that every historical company-held photo copy has already been destroyed.

Groups and older shared records

The completion-sharing control covers completed ticks, completion dates and the display name used in the group. It does not automatically share personal notes, ratings, shortlists, new photos or trips. Those categories need their own explicit sharing action where supported.

Older trips that a person explicitly shared may remain available to the group until that share is removed or the person leaves. The cloud photo objects and active photo records are now empty. Turning off completion sharing does not withdraw older trip records. Another person may also retain a copy of information they previously saw or saved. Choose carefully what you share.

New and edited Community recommendations remain private until reviewed and may take time to appear to other people. You can see your own recommendation while it waits for review. Approved recommendations show your chosen display name. Votes, ratings, reports and block choices are stored to operate Community. You can report or block content; repeated reports can hide a recommendation pending review. Do not post private or sensitive information there.

If the optional AI-assisted Community review route is enabled, each new post or edit requires its own unchecked consent choice. With your choice, RL Applications may send your chosen public display name, title, place, country, optional region/category, description and source URL, plus an opaque post ID and revision, as a task email through Resend to its Google Gmail review mailbox. The connected OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT agent may check current post text and primary sources, then automatically approve a low-risk suggestion or reject clear abuse; uncertain or reported content stays private for operator review. Your Wayfinder sign-in email, account UUID, Memories photos and photo metadata, purchases and reporter details are not included in the review task email. A source link can reveal personal details or tracking parameters, so do not submit a private link. AI-assisted review does not guarantee every place, claim or access condition is genuine. Rejected content stays private with an author-only reason. Appeal or request data access/deletion through help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Withdrawing or deleting a post cannot unsend a task email or instantly erase copies already held by providers.

Service providers and hosting

  • Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure. Its former Wayfinder photo bucket and active photo table are currently empty, and client photo uploads are blocked.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the current Wayfinder PWA.
  • Cloudflare Pages hosts this support and policy website. These hosting providers may receive normal connection information such as IP address and browser details.
  • BigDataCloud receives coordinates and your IP address when you consent to an optional Near me lookup. It may use free-service observations to improve IP geolocation.
  • Resend sends Wayfinder account confirmation and recovery email for Supabase. It receives the recipient email address, the authentication message and link or code, and delivery metadata needed to send and troubleshoot the message. The configured sending region is Tokyo (ap-northeast-1), but that setting does not mean all processing or storage stays in Japan. Open and click tracking are disabled. Resend does not receive Wayfinder photos through this account-email flow. Resend privacy policy.
  • Google email infrastructure provides the current Gmail support mailbox and carried messages sent to the previous public support address. Google may continue to store those historical messages. The Resend no-reply address is only an automated account sender and is not a monitored support mailbox.
  • Optional consented Community review, when enabled: Resend sends the limited post/display-name/source task email to the Google Gmail review mailbox, and OpenAI processes its text through the connected Codex/ChatGPT account. This is not the OpenAI Platform API; API retention defaults do not describe this connected account. Provider copies and retention depend on their applicable policies and account controls; no immediate cross-provider deletion is promised. No Wayfinder photo image or photo metadata enters this route. Resend privacy, Google privacy, OpenAI data controls.
  • Apple and RevenueCat support the native iOS purchase and restore path. The store listing is not live; when purchases become available, the account-linked identifier and purchase status are handled as described below.

Providers may process information outside Australia under their own infrastructure and terms. This website contains no advertising or analytics script and does not set advertising cookies.

Purchases and store status

Purchases are not active in the web review build, and Wayfinder is not publicly on sale in the App Store or Google Play. The prepared native iOS purchase and restore path uses Apple for payment and RevenueCat for the individual Wayfinder account's linked app-user identifier, purchase receipt information and entitlement status. Joining a group does not transfer or share a paid entitlement with its members. An Android store release is planned but not live. Apple and, if an Android release is enabled, Google may keep transaction records under their own policies. RL Applications does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

Deleting a Wayfinder account starts removal of its linked purchase-provider customer record. Creating another account with the same email does not recover the deleted account or its progress. Apple retains its transaction history; eligible purchases can be restored separately. Contact support if paid access is not restored. Account deletion does not itself issue a refund.

Optional reminders

Adventure reminders are off unless you enable them. They are generated on your device using your reminder settings.

Storage, security and retention

Wayfinder uses device storage for new photos, offline access and queued changes, and hosted services for accounts and progress syncing. No system can promise perfect security. Store purchases are not yet publicly available, and Community recommendations may wait for review before they are visible to others.

Hosted information is kept while needed to provide the service, resolve support or security issues, meet legal obligations and maintain proportionate provider backups. Precise production retention periods have not yet been finalised. The separately retained Desktop backup of historical photos is described above. Removing the app or clearing one browser can remove new device-local photos but does not itself delete hosted account data or the separately retained historical backup.

Your choices

  • Decline location permission and browse without Near me.
  • Choose whether to add a device-local photo, manually export/import your encrypted photo backup, share completed adventures with a group, or submit a Community recommendation for review.
  • Review or change personal content through the app where the relevant feature is operating.
  • Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com with a privacy, access or correction request, or use the account-deletion instructions for in-app and email deletion options.

Children

Wayfinder is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied personal information can email help.rlapplications@gmail.com.
