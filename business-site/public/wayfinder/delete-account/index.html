Account control

Delete your Wayfinder account

Use the deletion control inside Wayfinder. It is available for testing while the complete production result remains under verification.

Open WayfinderEmail a deletion request

The review build includes an in-app path under Me → Delete my account and all my data. Complete production deletion is still being verified. If you cannot use that control, the email-request route below is available.

Use the in-app control

  1. Open Wayfinder and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Me, then choose Delete my account and all my data.
  3. Read the confirmation carefully and continue only if you intend permanent deletion.

If you cannot sign in or have already uninstalled the app, email help.rlapplications@gmail.com, preferably from the address connected to the Wayfinder account, and say that you are requesting account deletion. RL Applications may ask for enough information to locate the account and verify that it belongs to you. Do not send a password, verification link, government identity document, payment-card number or app-store password.

What the request covers

A completed in-app deletion is intended to delete the Wayfinder account and remove associated personal data where RL Applications controls it, including:

  • authentication and profile records;
  • completion history, dates, shortlist choices, ratings and personal notes;
  • trips and older uploaded cloud photos controlled through Wayfinder;
  • group membership and sharing records;
  • Community submissions, votes, star ratings, reports and blocks associated with the account.

Limits and separate records

The in-app deletion flow also removes the account's new device-local photos from the device where deletion is run. It cannot remotely erase device-local photos on another phone, app installation or browser profile. Clear that app's data, clear the PWA's browser/site storage, or uninstall the native app to remove remaining local copies. Signing out alone preserves them for the same account.

Uninstalling Wayfinder or clearing one browser does not delete a hosted account. Information may remain in backups, security logs or records that must be kept for legal or fraud-prevention reasons, for the periods applicable to those systems or obligations. Precise production retention periods have not yet been finalised. People who previously received shared information may retain their own copies, and deletion cannot remove screenshots or other independent copies held by someone else.

If purchase-provider records exist, RL Applications will initiate the applicable provider deletion process. Apple and Google may retain transaction records under their own policies. Account deletion does not itself create a refund; any future store purchase and refund request will be handled under the applicable store’s rules. Purchases are not active in the current PWA review build.

If you only want help

Deletion is permanent once completed. If the problem is sign-in, syncing, a shared item or incorrect account information, read the Wayfinder support guidance or email help.rlapplications@gmail.com before deleting.
