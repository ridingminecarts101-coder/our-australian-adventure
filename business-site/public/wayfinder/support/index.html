Frequently asked questions

How do I install the PWA on my phone?

Wayfinder supports iOS and Android through the current PWA review build. The Wayfinder review build link opens the same app as Open Wayfinder on the overview page. You can use it in your browser or add it to your Home Screen.

iPhone and iPad — iOS and iPadOS

  1. Open the Wayfinder review build in Safari.
  2. Use Safari’s Share options to find Add to Home Screen. Depending on your device and browser layout, Share or Add to Home Screen may be inside a More menu.
  3. If shown, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Apple’s Home Screen guides: iPhone · iPad.

Android phones and tablets

  1. Open the Wayfinder review build in Chrome.
  2. Open the three-dot menu and choose Install and create shortcut → Install. Depending on your Chrome version, this may be labelled Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Follow the prompts, then open Wayfinder from your Home Screen.

Google’s web-app installation guide

Store availability: native iOS and Android releases are in preparation. Wayfinder is not yet published in the App Store or Google Play; use the PWA above for now.

What if my verification or recovery email does not arrive?

Account emails arrive as Wayfinder from no-reply@auth.rlapplications.com. Check that you entered the correct email address and look in your spam or junk folder. If you need another verification email, use Resend verification email in the app. For an expired or already-used link, request a fresh verification or recovery email and use the newest link.

Do not erase browser storage to work around an account prompt: it may contain unsynced progress and device-local photos. If the problem continues, email support with your device, browser and the error message. Never send a password or verification link. The no-reply address is an automated sender; use the support address below for help.

Can I use Wayfinder offline?

Previously loaded parts of the app can work offline. Progress and other queued account changes must reconnect before they can sync. New photos remain on the device and do not join that sync. Keep another copy of important photos or trip notes while the app is in testing.

Why do groups say maintenance?

Group sharing is temporarily unavailable while we test how personal progress is shared and kept private. Your own records should remain personal to your account. Community protections are also still being tested; do not post private or sensitive information there.

Will my progress work on another device?

Use the same verified account on each device. Signed-in personal progress is designed to sync across devices; sync and conflict handling remain under testing. First confirm that changes have synced on the original device. Photos added in current versions stay on that device and will not appear on another device, even with the same account.

Where are my new photos kept?

In the current device-local build, new photos are resized and kept only inside Wayfinder in the app or browser on the device where you add them. The native app uses private app files; it does not copy the Wayfinder version to the photo gallery. The PWA uses that browser's IndexedDB storage. This build does not upload the new image or its related photo record to the RL Applications backend or GitHub, and it does not sync them to another device. Until the matching server boundary is deployed, an older client may still use the former cloud-upload path. Signing out preserves device-local photos for the same account, but uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or clearing browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Wayfinder has no photo export or device-backup feature, so keep your own independent copy. Native releases exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. A PWA cannot control copies retained by browser, operating-system or user-configured device backups. Older cloud photos remain available read-only.

Can I buy Wayfinder packs?

No paid access is active in the PWA review build. The planned native-app model is AUD $14.99 once for all continents, including Antarctica, or AUD $2.99 for each of the seven standalone geography packs. Antarctica will only be in the all-continents bundle. Prices, availability and store handling remain subject to release setup and the applicable app store.

Does Wayfinder book travel or confirm an activity is open?

No. Wayfinder is a discovery and planning guide. Availability, access, prices, seasons, border rules and safety conditions can change. Confirm important details with official authorities, venues and operators before relying on them.

How do I report incorrect content or a safety concern?

Email rambodog555@gmail.com with the adventure name, country and what needs review. For a Community safety concern, include enough context to identify the item but do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How do I delete my account?

See the account-deletion instructions. The in-app path is still being verified, so email is the recommended fallback during testing.
