Wayfinder support

Help with accounts, saved data, existing testers and the path to release.

Email Wayfinder supportDelete an account

Wayfinder is still in testing. Email verification and password recovery are available through the automated account sender, and the public support mailbox is available. Groups are currently unavailable, Community protections are still being tested, and purchases are not active in the web review build. The in-app deletion control is available for testing with the limits described on the deletion page.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download Wayfinder?

Wayfinder is preparing an iPhone and iPad App Store release. The listing is not live yet, so the download action on the overview is inactive. We will add a verified App Store link once the app is available.

An Android release is planned, but Wayfinder is not published on Google Play either. We will add an Android download link only after its listing is live.

What if my verification or recovery email does not arrive?

Account emails arrive as Wayfinder from no-reply@auth.rlapplications.com. Check that you entered the correct email address and look in your spam or junk folder. If you need another verification email, use Resend verification email in the app. For an expired or already-used link, request a fresh verification or recovery email and use the newest link.

Do not erase browser storage to work around an account prompt: it may contain unsynced progress and device-local photos. Never send a password or verification link to the no-reply address; it is an automated sender and is not monitored for support. For further help, email help.rlapplications@gmail.com.

Can I use Wayfinder offline?

Previously loaded parts of the app can work offline. Progress and other queued account changes must reconnect before they can sync. New photos remain on the device and do not join that sync. Keep another copy of important photos or trip notes while the app is in testing.

Why do groups say maintenance?

Group sharing is temporarily unavailable while we test how personal progress is shared and kept private. Your own records should remain personal to your account. Community protections are also still being tested. New or edited recommendations are reviewed before appearing to others, and you can report or block content. Do not post private or sensitive information there.

Will my progress work on another device?

Use the same verified account on each device. Signed-in personal progress is designed to sync across devices; sync and conflict handling remain under testing. First confirm that changes have synced on the original device. Photos added in current versions stay on that device and will not appear on another device, even with the same account.

Where are my new photos kept?

New photos are resized and kept only inside Wayfinder in the app or browser on the device where you add them. The native app uses private app files; it does not copy the Wayfinder version to the photo gallery. The PWA uses that browser's IndexedDB storage. Current clients cannot upload new image files or photo records to the RL Applications backend, and the former cloud photo store is empty. Photos do not sync to another device. Signing out preserves device-local photos for the same account, but uninstalling the app, clearing app data, or clearing browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Wayfinder has no photo export or device-backup feature, so keep your own independent copy. Native releases exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. A PWA cannot control copies retained by browser, operating-system or user-configured device backups. RL Applications retains a separate Desktop backup of 15 historical originals and related migration snapshots; it is not available through Wayfinder and is retained for migration and rollback evidence pending a final retention decision.

Can I buy Wayfinder packs?

Not publicly yet. The PWA review build cannot sell packs. The native iOS build integrates Apple in-app purchases through RevenueCat, and eight products are configured for store preparation and testing, but the App Store listing is not live. The planned model is AUD $14.99 once for all continents, including Antarctica, or AUD $2.99 for each of the seven standalone geography packs. Antarctica is only in the all-continents bundle. Prices and availability remain subject to the store release.

Does Wayfinder book travel or confirm an activity is open?

No. Wayfinder is a discovery and planning guide. Availability, access, prices, seasons, border rules and safety conditions can change. Confirm important details with official authorities, venues and operators before relying on them.

How do I report incorrect content or a safety concern?

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com with the adventure name, what appears wrong and a current official source if you have one. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How do I delete my account?

See the account-deletion instructions. The in-app control is available for testing, but its complete production result is still being verified. The deletion page also provides an email-request route if you cannot use the in-app control.

Contact

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Include the problem, the device and browser or app version, and any error text that is safe to share. Do not send passwords, verification links, payment details or unnecessary identity documents. The automated no-reply sender cannot receive support messages.
