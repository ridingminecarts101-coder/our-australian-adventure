Wayfinder support

Help with accounts, saved data, existing testers and the path to release.

Email Wayfinder supportDelete an account

Wayfinder is preparing for release. A verified account is required to use Wayfinder. Account verification and password recovery emails and the public support mailbox are available. You can choose whether to share completions with a group and use the in-app account-deletion control. Community recommendations stay private until reviewed, which may take time; reporting and blocking are available. The web review build cannot sell packs, and the iOS App Store listing is not live.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download Wayfinder?

Wayfinder is preparing an iPhone and iPad App Store release. The listing is not live yet, so the download action on the overview is inactive. We will add a verified App Store link once the app is available.

An Android release is planned, but Wayfinder is not published on Google Play either. We will add an Android download link only after its listing is live.

What if my verification or recovery email does not arrive?

Account emails arrive as Wayfinder from no-reply@auth.rlapplications.com. Check that you entered the correct email address and look in your spam or junk folder. If you need another verification email, use Resend verification email in the app. For an expired or already-used link, request a fresh verification or recovery email and use the newest link.

Do not erase browser storage to work around an account prompt: it may contain unsynced progress and device-local photos. Never send a password or verification link to the no-reply address; it is an automated sender and is not monitored for support. For further help, email help.rlapplications@gmail.com.

Can I use Wayfinder offline?

Previously loaded parts of the app can work offline. Progress and other queued account changes must reconnect before they can sync. New photos remain on the device and do not join that sync. Keep another copy of important photos or trip notes while the app is in testing.

How does group sharing work?

Your progress belongs to your account. You can create or join a group and choose whether to share your completion ticks and dates with its members. Notes, ratings, shortlists, new device-local photos and trips are not shared by that completion setting. You can turn completion sharing off under Me; a separate trip share, if you made one, needs its own removal. Confirm what is visible before sharing personal information.

Will my progress work on another device?

Use the same verified account on each device. Signed-in personal progress syncs when the device reconnects; first confirm that changes have synced on the original device. New photos do not sync automatically. To move their real image bytes, open Me → Settings → Export photos from this device, save every numbered .wfpbackup file and transfer it yourself. Choose Import a photo backup while signed in to the same account on the other device. Keep the backup password because Wayfinder cannot recover an encrypted file if you forget it.

Where are my new photos kept?

New photos are resized and kept only inside Wayfinder in the app or browser on the device where you add them. The native app uses private app files; it does not copy the Wayfinder version to the photo gallery. The PWA uses that browser's IndexedDB storage. Current clients cannot upload new image files or photo records to the RL Applications backend, and the former cloud photo store is empty. Photos do not automatically sync to another device. Signing out preserves them for the same account, but uninstalling the app or clearing app or browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Under Me → Settings, manual export and import use password-encrypted, numbered .wfpbackup files for that same account. Save every part; RL Applications cannot recover a forgotten backup password. Wayfinder does not upload or automatically transfer these files. Files, AirDrop, email or a cloud provider may hold a separate copy if you choose that route. Native releases exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. A PWA cannot control copies retained by browser, operating-system or user-configured device backups. RL Applications retains a separate Desktop backup of 15 historical originals and related migration snapshots; it is not available through Wayfinder and is retained for migration and rollback evidence pending a final retention decision.

Can I buy Wayfinder packs?

Not publicly yet. The web review build cannot sell packs, and the iOS App Store listing is not live. The prepared native iOS model is AUD $14.99 once for all continents, including Antarctica, or AUD $2.99 for each of the seven standalone geography packs. Antarctica is only in the all-continents bundle. Apple and RevenueCat handle the native purchase and restore path for the individual Wayfinder account; joining a group does not give its members your paid packs. Prices and availability remain subject to the store release.

Does Wayfinder book travel or confirm an activity is open?

No. Wayfinder is a discovery and planning guide. Availability, access, prices, seasons, border rules and safety conditions can change. Confirm important details with official authorities, venues and operators before relying on them.

How do I report incorrect content or a safety concern?

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com with the adventure name, what appears wrong and a current official source if you have one. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information.

How are Community suggestions reviewed?

New posts and edits stay private until reviewed. When the optional AI-assisted route is enabled, you must check a fresh, initially unchecked consent box for each submission or edit before its public display name, text and source link can be emailed through Resend to RL Applications' Gmail review mailbox and processed by a connected OpenAI Codex/ChatGPT agent. The agent may approve a low-risk suggestion or reject clear abuse after checking the current post. Uncertain or reported suggestions stay private for operator review. Your account sign-in email and Memories photos do not enter the review task email. A rejection stays private with a reason visible only to you under Community → Mine; request another review at help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Review can take time and does not guarantee that a venue or activity is open.

How do I delete my account?

See the account-deletion instructions. The in-app control deletes the account and owned hosted Wayfinder records and removes new device-local photos on the device where you use it. Creating another account with the same email does not recover the deleted account or its progress. Apple retains its transaction history; eligible purchases can be restored separately. Contact support if paid access is not restored. The control cannot erase independent copies on other devices, exported photo backup files or provider records immediately. The deletion page also gives an email-request route if you cannot use the in-app control.

Contact

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Include the problem, the device and browser or app version, and any error text that is safe to share. Do not send passwords, verification links, payment details or unnecessary identity documents. The automated no-reply sender cannot receive support messages.
