Testing notice

What to expect from this build

The current Wayfinder PWA is published for review. The adventure catalogue, core browsing, email verification and password recovery are available. Group features, Community safeguards, purchases and end-to-end deletion are still being prepared or verified. Group controls may show maintenance.

Please avoid posting sensitive material or relying on the testing build as the only copy of important photos or trip notes. Wayfinder is an inspiration and planning guide; it does not book travel, guarantee access or replace official safety, visa, health or operator advice.
