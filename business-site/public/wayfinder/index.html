What to expect

Wayfinder is not yet available in the App Store or Google Play. The adventure catalogue, core browsing, email verification and password recovery are in the current review build. Group features, Community safeguards, purchases and end-to-end deletion are still being prepared or verified. Group controls may show maintenance for existing testers.

For existing testers, please avoid posting sensitive material or relying on the review build as the only copy of important photos or trip notes. Wayfinder is an inspiration and planning guide; it does not book travel, guarantee access or replace official safety, visa, health or operator advice.
