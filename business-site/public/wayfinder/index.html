What to expect

Wayfinder is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store; an Android release is planned. A verified account is required to use Wayfinder. It lets you browse adventures, save progress, recover your account, choose whether to share completions with a group, report or block Community content, and delete your account in the app. New and edited Community recommendations stay private until reviewed and may take time to appear to others. Optional iOS packs are purchased through Apple; the web review build cannot sell packs.

Keep another copy of important photos or trip notes. Wayfinder is an inspiration and planning guide; it does not book travel, guarantee access or replace official safety, visa, health or operator advice.
