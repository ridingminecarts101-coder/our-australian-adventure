Your rental record, in your hands

TenancyTrove helps Australian renters record a tenancy, inspect rooms, keep original files, prepare evidence packs and find official rental help. All current tools are free, and records stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

TenancyTrove 1.0.0 has been submitted to Apple for review. A public App Store download will follow approval and release.
