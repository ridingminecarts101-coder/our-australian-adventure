Privacy

Wayfinder · last updated 14 September 2026

Wayfinder is a travel checklist. It keeps the places you want to go, the ones you have been to, and the photos you attach to them. This page says exactly what that means for your data, in plain terms.

Testing status: Email verification and password recovery are available. Group sharing is currently unavailable, Community protections are still being tested, and purchases are not active in the web review build. Avoid posting sensitive material and keep another copy of important photos and trip notes. See the current Wayfinder privacy supplement for testing updates.

Accounts and advertising

  • An email and password account is required. Your display name does not need to be your legal name.
  • Wayfinder does not run advertising or add advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Service providers process information needed to operate the app, as described below.
  • Your email and password are not shown to group members or Community readers.

Location

The Near me button asks for your location when you press it. With your permission, your current coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud to find your region. That provider also receives your IP address and explains that its free service uses these observations to improve IP geolocation. Wayfinder does not save these coordinates in your account or track your route. You can browse all destinations without granting location access. How BigDataCloud uses this request.

Photos

Photos are read only when you choose them. In the current device-local build, a new photo is resized and kept only on the device where you add it. In the native app it is stored in Wayfinder's private app files, not copied to the phone's photo gallery. In the PWA it is stored in that browser's IndexedDB storage. Wayfinder does not upload the new image or its related photo record to the RL Applications backend or GitHub. New photos do not sync, so signing in on another device will not show them. Until the matching server boundary is deployed, an older client may still use Wayfinder's former cloud-upload path.

Signing out keeps new device-local photos for the same account and hides them from other signed-in accounts. Uninstalling the native app, clearing its app data, or clearing the PWA's browser/site storage can permanently remove them. Wayfinder does not currently provide a photo export or device-backup feature, so keep an independent copy of anything important. Native releases exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data, including new photos, from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. The PWA cannot control copies a browser, operating system or user-configured device backup may retain outside its site storage.

Photos uploaded by an earlier version remain readable from Wayfinder's private, non-publicly-listable cloud bucket through short-lived signed links. Group membership alone does not share new photos. An older photo previously shared with a group may remain visible to its members until you leave that group. Stopping completion sharing affects ticks; it does not withdraw previously shared photos or trip plans.

Traveller recommendations

Anything you post to the Community list is public to everyone using the app, along with the display name you have set. Your votes and star ratings are private — the totals are public, but nobody can see who voted which way. Reports you make are visible only to us, and blocking somebody is private to you. Community protections and complete account-deletion behaviour are still being tested; do not post private or sensitive information there.

Personal progress and groups

Your progress belongs to your account. Group sharing is currently unavailable while its ownership and privacy controls are prepared. When available, a group will be optional and joining will use an invitation code. You will choose whether to share completed adventures. If enabled, the group can see past and future completion ticks, completion dates and your display name. This choice does not share private notes, ratings, shortlist, new photos or trip plans.

When group sharing is available, its intended controls let you change completion sharing under Me → Sharing and switch between Personal and Group views. Leaving is intended to remove your membership and shared entries from that group while keeping your own records. It does not copy other members' achievements into your account. Older shared lists retain their existing sharing choices when upgraded. Someone who has already viewed shared information may have kept a separate copy.

Purchases

Purchases are not active in the web review build. If one-time hidden-gem packs are enabled in a future native release, they will belong to an individual Wayfinder account; joining a group will not give other members your purchases. Apple or Google will handle payment details, and Wayfinder will not receive your card number. RevenueCat is planned to process your Wayfinder account identifier, purchase and receipt information, unlocked packs and technical information needed to operate billing. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Notifications

Optional, off unless you turn them on, and generated on your own device. They fire at most once a week and only for shortlisted adventures that are in season. No notification content leaves your phone.

Deleting your account

The review build includes Me → Delete my account and all my data, which requires a connection and confirmation. Complete removal of associated hosted files and service-provider records is still being verified. Manual email requests are temporarily unavailable while a support address is assigned. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

Use the Wayfinder account-deletion page for the current request status and guidance. Once manual requests are available, we will verify account ownership and confirm completion only after the relevant data and service-provider steps have been checked. A request is intended to cover the account, personal progress, older uploaded cloud photos, trips, group memberships and Community contributions. Store transaction records and copies another person independently saved are separate; account deletion does not itself issue a refund.

The in-app deletion flow removes the account's new device-local photos from the device where it runs. An emailed or server-side deletion request cannot remotely erase local photos on another device or browser profile. Clear that app's data, clear the PWA's browser/site storage, or uninstall the native app to remove any remaining local copies. Signing out alone preserves them.

Retention and device copies

Your hosted travel records remain until you remove them or delete your account. Local caches let a previously signed-in device work offline. Signing out clears the account-data cache but preserves account-scoped device-local photos; other offline devices may still hold earlier records until they reconnect or their app data is cleared. Service-provider backup and operational records may persist beyond removal from the active app. The new support address will be published here for retention enquiries and privacy requests.

Children

Wayfinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Travel advice is not our advice

Some countries carry a travel warning inside the app. It is a convenience, not a source of truth, and it can be out of date. Always check your own government's current advice before booking anything. Opening hours, access, permits, seasonal closures and Traditional Owner requirements also change; check before you travel.
