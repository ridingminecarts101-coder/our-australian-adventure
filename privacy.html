Privacy

Wayfinder · last updated 4 September 2026

Wayfinder is a travel checklist. It keeps the places you want to go, the ones you have been to, and the photos you attach to them. This page says exactly what that means for your data, in plain terms.

What is not

  • No name, email address or phone number is required to use the app.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • No analytics service. Nobody is watching what you tap.
  • Your data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone.

Location

The Near me button asks for your location only at the moment you press it. The coordinates are used once, to work out which region you are in, and are not stored or sent anywhere except to a geocoding service that turns them into a place name. The app works entirely without ever granting it.

Photos

Photos are read only when you choose them. They are resized on your device before upload, and stored in a private bucket that is not publicly listable — the app fetches them through short-lived signed links. They are visible to you and, if you are in a shared group, to the other people in that group. Nobody else.

Sharing a list

A group is how two or more phones keep one list. Joining needs a code that somebody in the group reads out to you. While you are in a group, the others can see the ticks, photos, trips and display name in it. Leaving a group stops that, and removes only you.

Notifications

Optional, off unless you turn them on, and generated on your own device. They fire at most once a week and only for shortlisted adventures that are in season. No notification content leaves your phone.

Deleting everything

Me → Delete my account and all my data. It removes your account, every tick, every trip and every photo file, immediately and permanently. There is no soft delete and no recovery, so be sure. You do not need to email anyone or wait for a reply.

Who runs it

Wayfinder is an independent app. The database and file storage are hosted by Supabase, and the app itself is served by GitHub Pages; both process data on our behalf as infrastructure providers.

Children

Wayfinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Travel advice is not our advice

Some countries carry a travel warning inside the app. It is a convenience, not a source of truth, and it can be out of date. Always check your own government's current advice before booking anything. Opening hours, access, permits, seasonal closures and Traditional Owner requirements also change; check before you travel.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it, and the app will say so on next launch.
