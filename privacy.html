Privacy

Wayfinder · last updated 11 September 2026

Wayfinder is a travel checklist. It keeps the places you want to go, the ones you have been to, and the photos you attach to them. This page says exactly what that means for your data, in plain terms.

Accounts and advertising

  • An email and password account is required. Your display name does not need to be your legal name.
  • Wayfinder does not run advertising or add advertising trackers.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal data. Service providers process information needed to operate the app, as described below.
  • Your email and password are not shown to group members or Community readers.

Location

The Near me button asks for your location when you press it. With your permission, your current coordinates are sent to BigDataCloud to find your region. That provider also receives your IP address and explains that its free service uses these observations to improve IP geolocation. Wayfinder does not save these coordinates in your account or track your route. You can browse all destinations without granting location access. How BigDataCloud uses this request.

Photos

Photos are read only when you choose them. They are resized on your device before upload, and stored in a private bucket that is not publicly listable — the app fetches them through short-lived signed links. Group membership alone does not share newly added photos. Photos previously shared with a group may remain visible to its members until you leave that group. Stopping completion sharing affects ticks; it does not withdraw previously shared photos or trip plans.

Traveller recommendations

Anything you post to the Community list is public to everyone using the app, along with the display name you have set. Your votes and star ratings are private — the totals are public, but nobody can see who voted which way. Reports you make are visible only to us, and blocking somebody is private to you. You can delete your own posts at any time, and deleting your account removes them all.

Personal progress and groups

Your progress belongs to your account. A group is optional, and joining uses an invitation code. When joining, you choose whether to share your completed adventures. If enabled, the group can see your past and future completion ticks, completion dates and display name. This choice does not share your private notes, ratings, shortlist, new photos or trip plans.

You can change completion sharing under Me → Sharing, or switch between Personal and Group views. Leaving removes your membership and shared entries from that group while keeping your own records. It does not copy other members' achievements into your account. Older shared lists retain their existing sharing choices when upgraded. Someone who has already viewed shared information may have kept a separate copy.

Purchases

Hidden-gem packs are one-time purchases for an individual Wayfinder account. Joining a group does not give other members your purchases. Apple or Google handles payment details; Wayfinder does not receive your card number. RevenueCat processes your Wayfinder account identifier, purchase and receipt information, unlocked packs and technical information needed to operate billing. RevenueCat privacy policy.

Notifications

Optional, off unless you turn them on, and generated on your own device. They fire at most once a week and only for shortlisted adventures that are in season. No notification content leaves your phone.

Deleting your account

Me → Delete my account and all my data. It removes your account, personal progress, uploaded photos, trips, group memberships and Community contributions. It requires a connection and confirmation. It cannot be undone; uninstalling the app alone does not delete your account.

If you cannot use the app, you can request account and data deletion through Support. We verify that the request comes from the account owner before acting. Deleting Wayfinder data does not itself issue a store refund or erase Apple's or Google's transaction records. Purchase-provider records require a separate provider deletion step; contact Support for help with that request. We cannot remove copies another person has independently saved.

Supabase hosts account authentication, the database and photo storage. GitHub Pages serves the website. Authentication emails are delivered through the configured email service. RevenueCat and the app stores support native purchases; BigDataCloud handles optional location lookups. These services receive the information needed for their role, including normal network request information, and may process it outside Australia.

Retention and device copies

Your saved travel records remain until you remove them or delete your account. Local caches let a previously signed-in device work offline. Signing out clears that device's account cache; other offline devices may still hold earlier copies until they reconnect or their app data is cleared. Service-provider backup and operational records may persist beyond removal from the active app. Contact us for the applicable retention details or a privacy request.

Children

Wayfinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Travel advice is not our advice

Some countries carry a travel warning inside the app. It is a convenience, not a source of truth, and it can be out of date. Always check your own government's current advice before booking anything. Opening hours, access, permits, seasonal closures and Traditional Owner requirements also change; check before you travel.
