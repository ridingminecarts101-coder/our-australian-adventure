### Turkmenistan (TM)

Smartraveller currently advises exercising a high degree of caution. A visa and State Migration Service letter of invitation are required even for transit; regional road travel and foreigner access can be restricted, while entry conditions and borders may change without notice. The Afghan border region is do-not-travel.

Both proposed sites are away from the Afghan border, but neither should be presented as independently accessible. The descriptions require approved routing and pre-arranged transport. Re-check the visa, route permit and operator status before integration. Source opened and read: https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/asia/turkmenistan (accessed 2026-09-11).
