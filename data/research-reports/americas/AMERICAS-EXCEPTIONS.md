# Americas coordinator exception report

Audited 11 September 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

## Country exceptions

- **Haiti (HT): 5 rows / 0 gems; exact deficit 2.** No new bookable-sounding
  experience was added while Australian Smartraveller says do not travel due to
  violent crime, kidnapping, severe shortages and volatile access. Existing
  rows are preserved for primary editorial quarantine/review.
- **Venezuela (VE): 7 rows / 0 gems; exact deficit 2.** No paid discoveries were
  added while Smartraveller says do not travel and describes violent crime,
  political/economic instability, kidnapping, arbitrary detention and severely
  limited consular support. Current evidence did not justify implying reliable
  visitor access.
- **Suriname (SR): 6 rows / 1 gem; exact deficit 1.** Bigi Pan was retained with
  a current government source and explicit operator/access checks. Research did
  not find a second specific current primary page with enough visitor-access
  detail to support another paid experience without filler.

These exceptions do not prevent the existing North America and South America
packs from exceeding 20%. They are deliberate quality and safety decisions,
not lint waivers.

## Access, cost and source corrections

- Cuba and Nicaragua rows retain prominent live-advice and guide/access checks;
  no row promises future availability or safety.
- Guadeloupe's Habitation La Grivelière is closed for renovation. The retained
  row covers only Grande Rivière valley, which the same tourism-authority page
  says remains accessible.
- Saint Pierre's L'Arche museum is closed for building works and was removed
  from the batch. The replacement, Musée Héritage, has a current exact page and
  a June–September season.
- A dead Cayman tourism PDF was replaced with current National Trust pages.
- Mainland fee review corrected Mushpot Cave and Florissant Fossil Beds'
  Petrified Forest Loop from free to `cost: 1`; their current NPS fee pages were
  opened. Remaining free labels were re-audited against admission and required
  transport.
- Automated URL checks can be blocked even when a page is readable in a normal
  browser. The mainland batch records one Yukon 403; the Caribbean/Central
  batch records two 403s (INGUAT and Discover Puerto Rico). Those pages were
  separately opened/read. The reports do not treat status alone as evidence.

## Editorial cautions

- `cost` and `difficulty` are Wayfinder ordinal bands, not quoted prices or
  guarantees. `dog_friendly: check` is used where the source did not establish
  a stable rule. Travellers still need live checks for permits, weather,
  transport, land access, guide availability and closures.
- Culturally sensitive and community-hosted places are described as visits
  requiring local/site-authority interpretation, not spectacle.
- The fuzzy duplicate audit deliberately retained similarly named but distinct
  places: Alaska's Great Kobuk Sand Dunes versus Colorado's Great Sand Dunes,
  and Florissant's Colorado fossil-stump loop versus Arizona's Petrified Forest
  National Park.
- No external accounts, canonical/generated/native assets, SQL, billing,
  release branches or publishing targets were changed by this task.
