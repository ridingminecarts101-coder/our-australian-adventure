# South America evidence and access exceptions

Audited 11 September 2026 (Australia/Sydney).

## Venezuela (VE) — no additions; two-row deficit remains

Australia's Smartraveller advice was current on 11 September 2026 and said **do not travel** because of the dangerous security situation, violent crime, political and economic instability, kidnapping and arbitrary detention. It also reported severely limited Australian consular assistance, damaged infrastructure after the June earthquakes, limited flight operations and the possibility of border or movement restrictions changing at short notice.

Source opened: Australian Government, Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade, [Venezuela Travel Advice & Safety](https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/venezuela).

Inparques publishes general permit and park-use material, but that is not sufficient evidence that a specific memory-making experience is safely and reliably accessible to the app's likely Australian users now. Adding two paid gems would imply practical availability that the current evidence does not support. The seven existing canonical Venezuela rows are preserved, but none is reclassified. Revisit only after the Australian advice materially improves and current park/operator access can be independently confirmed.

## Suriname (SR) — one addition; one-row deficit remains

Bigi Pan is supported by a current Suriname government tourism-development report and government conservation material. The same 2026 report says management, coordination and protection remain unresolved, so the row explicitly tells travellers to verify operator and access arrangements.

Sources opened: Government of Suriname, [Management of Bigi Pan central to tourism stakeholders meeting](https://gov.sr/beheer-bigi-pan-centraal-tijdens-stakeholdersmeeting-toerisme-in-nickerie-en-coronie/) and [Nature-management service](https://gov.sr/ministeries/ministerie-van-grond-en-bosbeheer/de-dienst-s-lands-bosbeheer-lbb/).

The available primary material named other reserves and geographic superlatives but did not establish a second specific, presently bookable visitor experience with enough access detail. Wonotobo, Tafelberg and several nature reserves were therefore not converted into paid content merely to meet the ratio. Suriname remains at 16.67% pending a current park/community/operator page that can support a concrete experience.

## Evidence cautions retained in rows

- French Guiana: the Parc amazonien de Guyane trail map provides unusually precise route evidence but dates from 2017. Stable place/route claims are retained; every description tells travellers to check live track conditions with the park or local tourism office.
- Ecuador: the Ministry of Environment Amazon protected-area guide provides named Yasuní visitor sites, activities, controlled hours and an advance-reservation rule for Jatuncocha. Rows avoid promising wildlife sightings and require authorised itineraries.
- Colombia: additions are confined to named official tourism-board destinations and include current park, community and regional-security checks rather than blanket assurances.
- Falkland Islands: additions use the Falkland Islands Tourist Board's walking guidance and require land-access and countryside-code checks.
- Mutable cost/difficulty fields are Wayfinder editorial ordinals. Seasons are broad planning windows and do not replace live conditions, permits, closures or operator confirmation.

## Review note

No source was treated as verified merely because it appeared in a search result. The retained provenance records correspond to pages or primary documents opened and read during this audit. Booking aggregators and generic organiser itineraries were not used as evidence.
