Adding bookable-sounding adventures would mislead users. Source: https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/haiti. - **Cuba (CU): conditional content.** Smartraveller’s Cuba page, opened 2026-09-11, says reconsider travel because fuel shortages, blackouts and shortages are disrupting transport and essential services. Records specify guided/local arrangements and make no safety guarantee. Source: https://www.smartraveller.gov.au/destinations/americas/nicaragua.
