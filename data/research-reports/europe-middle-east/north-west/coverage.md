## Administrative balance

The additions deliberately strengthen thin areas: Northern Ireland in GB; Mayo, Offaly and Roscommon in IE; Saarland, Bremen and Brandenburg in DE; Burgenland in AT; Ticino, Uri and Graubunden in CH; North Brabant and Utrecht in NL; West Flanders in BE; Zealand and North Jutland in DK; Nordland and Innlandet in NO; Gotland, Kalmar and Skane in SE; Southwest Finland and Ostrobothnia in FI; the Westfjords and Eastern Region in IS; Parnu and Laane in EE; Kurzeme and Latgale in LV; Telsiai, Panevezys and Utena in LT; and Balzers/Ruggell as regions under the canonical Liechtenstein admin value.

France necessarily has several two-row clusters because the official unusual-sites collection contains strong, non-overlapping choices in Hauts-de-France and Bourgogne-Franche-Comte. Belgium has three Limburg rows, but each is independently routable and materially different (industrial interpretation, cycling infrastructure, sensory trail); the fourth specifically strengthens West Flanders.
