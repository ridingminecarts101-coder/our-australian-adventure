# North and west Europe exceptions and limits

Verified: 2026-09-11.

No country required a quota exception. All 18 assigned countries reach the coordinator-approved integer minimum and at least 20% hidden-gem coverage. Liechtenstein reaches 28.57% because one addition would leave it below target and two is the smallest passing integer.

## Access, safety and seasonality

- Wadden Sea mudflat walking should be undertaken with an authorised guide because tides can cut off return routes. The row does not imply that independent crossing is safe.
- Víðgelmir is presented as a managed cave visit, not an unrestricted cave route. Bull Rock and Lough Hyne are operator-, sea-state- and weather-dependent experiences.
- Bangor Trail, Rago National Park, Femundsmarka, Hornstrandir and several Nordic island or sea-kayak experiences involve remote terrain, limited services or boat/weather dependency. Descriptions avoid promises of routine access.
- Aursjøvegen and the Dalatangi approach are seasonal or condition-sensitive roads. Travellers must check current road and weather information.
- Besser Reef uses an August–March season to avoid the published April to mid-July bird-protection closure. Other wildlife and island rows use conservative seasons and do not guarantee sightings or transport.
- `dog_friendly: "check"` is retained wherever the primary source did not establish a durable rule. Costs are broad ordinal estimates, not price quotations. Coordinates are intentionally `null` rather than inferred.

## Quality limits

- Every proposed row is a distinct destination or independently bookable/waymarked experience. Famous Hraunfossar and Stuðlagil candidates were removed during review and replaced by the Stone Arch in Jafnadalur and Sænautasel turf farm. Token subactivities of existing flagship experiences were rejected.
- Descriptions avoid unsupported rankings and volatile operational promises. Official visitor, public-land, municipal, museum or destination-management pages were used as primary evidence; booking and organiser pages were not used as final provenance.
- Provenance records only URLs that were opened and read on 2026-09-11. A source supports the identity and defining feature of its matching row; mutable details remain deliberately conservative.
