## ID-preservation analysis

Canonical IDs are keyed through `data/ids.json` by `COUNTRY|place`. The proposed treatments alter only prose (title or description) and must retain the exact `country`, `place` and existing numeric `id`. Changing a place string, even to improve spelling or specificity, would require a separate migration review. This handoff therefore supplies recommendations only and does not rewrite canonical rows.

Other numeric or jurisdictional superlatives may be correct, but they should be retained only after a dedicated primary-source verification pass. Absence from this table is not proof that a claim has been verified.
