# VA starter coverage

This is a separate quality-led proposal for the canonical lead’s registry decision. It does not modify `additions.jsonl`.

| Code | Baseline VA rows | Proposed rows | Proposed gems | Gem share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| VA | 0 | 3 | 1 | 33.3% |

The starter deliberately stops at three distinct experiences. One is a genuinely restricted-access heritage site; the other two are a separately entered archaeological area and a functioning microstate service. Three rows make 33.3% the smallest achievable share above 20%. Adding museum-room tasks, another St Peter’s Square event, or a subactivity of the Basilica/Gardens would inflate the count with token duplicates.

All rows retain `continent: "Europe"`; the single gem uses `pack: "europe"`, while free/core rows correctly use `pack: null`.
