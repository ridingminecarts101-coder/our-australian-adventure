Common questions

How do I use the same progress on another phone?

Create a Wayfinder email and password account, verify the email, then sign in to that same account on your other device. Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen if needed. Existing users are asked to add recovery to their current account so its identity and records are preserved.

How do two phones share one list?

On the first phone: Me → Sharing → Create a group. It shows a join code. On the other phone: Me → Sharing → Join with a code, and type it in. Each person uses their own account and chooses whether to share completed adventures. Agreeing shares past and future ticks, with your name attached. Private notes, ratings and shortlist are kept personal.

Can I keep my own list while I am in a group?

Yes. Use the Personal / Group control near the adventure dice. Your personal progress remains yours. Under Me → Sharing you can start or stop sharing your completion ticks later. Leaving a group removes your membership and shared entries there, while keeping your own progress.

“Ticked by” shows the wrong name

Check that each person is signed in to their own account, then set your display name under Me → Settings → Change name. Reconnect the other devices to refresh names. If a completion comes from an older shared account, contact us: a display-name change cannot reliably recover who originally did it.

My two phones disagree about what is ticked

Ticks sync live, but a phone that has been in a pocket for a while can drop the connection. Opening the app reconnects it. If they still disagree, Me → Settings → Force refresh from server.

A photo will not attach

iPhone photos in HEIC format are handled, but a very large photo on a slow connection can take a moment. It is queued on the device and uploads by itself — keep or reopen the app with a connection so the upload can finish. Photos are resized before upload, but uploads still use mobile data when connected that way.

Does it work without signal?

After signing in on that device and loading the list, you can browse and save progress without signal. Changes queue until you reconnect. First sign-in, account recovery, group changes, purchases and account deletion need a connection. Previously unseen photos may also need a connection to load.

How do I delete everything?

Me → Delete my account and all my data, while online. Read the confirmation before continuing. If you cannot access the app, use the deletion request below. Uninstalling does not delete your account.

Do purchases cover my whole group?

No. Packs belong to one Wayfinder account. The Australian base prices are AUD $14.99 once for every continent pack or AUD $2.99 once for an individual continent pack. The store's confirmation shows the actual local price. Sign in to the same Wayfinder account and use Restore purchases when needed. Contact us before buying again if a pack you bought does not appear.

Does a pack purchase pay for the adventures?

A purchase unlocks Wayfinder guide content for your account. Travel, admission, equipment and guide fees are separate. The all-continents bundle also includes Antarctica; it does not book or pay for an expedition.

Are adventure costs and visit times live quotes?

Cost bands, durations and difficulty are planning estimates. Transport, equipment, guide requirements and your route can change the total. Check current operator prices, access, weather and travel advice before booking or setting out.

A place in the app has closed, moved or is wrong

Email the adventure name and what changed. Access, opening hours, permits and seasonal closures move around, and corrections are welcome.

What are traveller recommendations?

A separate list of places other people using Wayfinder have suggested and voted on. They are not checked by us and never join the main adventure list — different things, kept apart on purpose.

Something on the Community list is wrong or offensive

Press Report on the post. Three reports hide it from everyone while it is looked at. You can also press Block, which hides that person's posts from your view. If it is urgent, email us and it will be removed by hand.

Request Wayfinder account and data deletion

You can make this request without reinstalling Wayfinder. Email rambodog555@gmail.com with the subject Wayfinder account deletion, preferably from the address on your Wayfinder account. Tell us that you want the account and its associated data deleted. Do not send your password or identity documents.

We will verify ownership and confirm the outcome. The request covers your account, progress, photos, trips, group memberships and Community contributions, plus purchase-provider data we control. Store transaction records and independently saved copies are separate; deleting an account does not issue a refund. See Privacy for data and retention details.

Why does a country say “do not travel”?

Some countries carry a warning where the honest advice is not when to go but whether to. It is a convenience and it can be out of date — always check your own government's current travel advice before booking.
