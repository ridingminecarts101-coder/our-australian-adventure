Common questions

How do I use the same progress on another phone?

Use the same verified account on both phones. Signed-in progress is designed to sync across devices; sync and conflict handling remain under testing. First confirm that changes have synced on the original device. Photos added in current versions stay on that device and do not sync to another phone, even when you use the same account.

How do two phones share one list?

Group sharing is currently unavailable while we prepare and test its ownership and privacy controls. When it returns, each person will use their own account and choose whether to share completed adventures. Private notes, ratings and shortlist are intended to remain personal.

Can I keep my own list while I am in a group?

That is the intended design: personal progress belongs to your account, and group sharing is optional. The Personal / Group and sharing controls are currently unavailable while that behaviour is being tested.

“Ticked by” shows the wrong name

Check that each person is signed in to their own account, then set your display name under Me → Settings → Change name. Reconnect the other devices to refresh names. If a completion comes from an older shared account, a display-name change cannot reliably recover who originally did it.

My two phones disagree about what is ticked

Ticks sync live, but a phone that has been in a pocket for a while can drop the connection. Opening the app reconnects it. If they still disagree, Me → Settings → Force refresh from server.

A photo will not attach

Wayfinder reads a photo only after you choose it and resizes a copy on the device. A large photo can take a moment to process. If attaching it fails, keep the original and try again with free storage available.

Where is an attached photo stored?

In the current device-local build, new photos stay only inside Wayfinder on the device where you add them. The native app uses private app files and does not copy the Wayfinder version to your gallery; the PWA uses that browser's IndexedDB storage. Wayfinder does not upload the new image or its related photo record to the RL Applications backend or GitHub, and it does not sync to another device. The production backend now blocks client photo-record and cloud-file uploads. Signing out preserves them for the same account, while uninstalling, clearing app data, or clearing browser/site storage can remove them permanently. There is no Wayfinder photo export or device-backup feature, so keep an independent copy. Native releases exclude Wayfinder's persistent app data from Android and iOS device or cloud backups. A PWA cannot control copies retained by browser, operating-system or user-configured device backups. The active cloud-photo store is empty. RL Applications retains a separate Desktop backup of 15 historical originals and migration snapshots pending a final retention decision; those copies are not available through Wayfinder.

Does it work without signal?

After signing in on that device and loading the list, you can browse and save progress without signal. Changes queue until you reconnect. First sign-in, account recovery, group changes, purchases and account deletion need a connection. Device-local photos on this device remain available after signing in to the same account, subject to local storage still being present.

How do I delete everything?

The review build includes Me → Delete my account and all my data, while online, but complete removal of associated data is still being verified. During testing, use the account-deletion instructions, including if you cannot access the app. Uninstalling does not delete your account.

Do purchases cover my whole group?

Purchases are not active in the web review build. The planned native model is that packs belong to one Wayfinder account. The Australian base prices are AUD $14.99 once for every continent pack or AUD $2.99 once for an individual continent pack. The store will show the actual local price if purchases are enabled in a future native release.

Does a pack purchase pay for the adventures?

If purchases are enabled in a future native release, a purchase will unlock Wayfinder guide content for your account. Travel, admission, equipment and guide fees are separate. The all-continents bundle is planned to include Antarctica; it will not book or pay for an expedition.

Are adventure costs and visit times live quotes?

Cost bands, durations and difficulty are planning estimates. Transport, equipment, guide requirements and your route can change the total. Check current operator prices, access, weather and travel advice before booking or setting out.

A place in the app has closed, moved or is wrong

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com with the adventure name and a note of what changed. Access, opening hours, permits and seasonal closures can change; check current information before travelling.

What are traveller recommendations?

A separate list of places other people using Wayfinder have suggested and voted on. They are not checked by us and never join the main adventure list — different things, kept apart on purpose.

Something on the Community list is wrong or offensive

Press Report on the post. Three reports hide it from everyone pending review. You can also press Block, which hides that person's posts from your view. Community protections and the review process are still being tested. Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com if further help is needed; avoid sharing private or sensitive information.

Request Wayfinder account and data deletion

Deletion-request email: help.rlapplications@gmail.com. The review build includes Me → Delete my account and all my data while online, but complete removal of associated data is still being verified. The account-deletion page explains the current route and limits. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

For a manual request, RL Applications will verify ownership and confirm completion only after the relevant data and service-provider steps have been checked. The request is intended to cover your account, progress, trips, group memberships and Community contributions. Store transaction records and independently saved copies are separate; deleting an account does not issue a refund. See the Wayfinder privacy supplement for current data and retention information.

The in-app deletion flow also removes the account's device-local photos from the device where it runs. An emailed request cannot remotely erase local photos on another phone or browser profile. Clear its app data or browser/site storage, or uninstall the native app, to remove those copies; signing out alone preserves them.

Why does a country say “do not travel”?

Some countries carry a warning where the honest advice is not when to go but whether to. It is a convenience and it can be out of date — always check your own government's current travel advice before booking.
