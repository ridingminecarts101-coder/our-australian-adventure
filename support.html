Common questions

How do two phones share one list?

On the first phone: Me → Sharing → Create a group. It shows a join code. On the other phone: Me → Sharing → Join with a code, and type it in. Everything already on either phone comes with it.

A third person joined and things went strange

That was a real bug, fixed in the September 2026 update. Before it, every phone signed in as the same account, so the app could not tell three people apart — and one person leaving the group removed it for everybody. Update the app and the group will behave.

“Ticked by” shows the wrong name

Set your name under Me → Settings → Change name. It now applies to everything you have ever ticked, on every phone in the group, straight away.

My two phones disagree about what is ticked

Ticks sync live, but a phone that has been in a pocket for a while can drop the connection. Opening the app reconnects it. If they still disagree, Me → Settings → Force refresh from server.

A photo will not attach

iPhone photos in HEIC format are handled, but a very large photo on a slow connection can take a moment. It is queued on the device and uploads by itself — you can close the app. Photos are resized before upload, so they will not eat your data allowance.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. The whole list works offline, and anything you tick is queued and sent when you are back online. Photos queue the same way.

How do I delete everything?

Me → Delete my account and all my data. It is immediate and permanent — every tick, trip and photo. No email, no waiting.

A place in the app has closed, moved or is wrong

Email the adventure name and what changed. Access, opening hours, permits and seasonal closures move around, and corrections are welcome.

Why does a country say “do not travel”?

Some countries carry a warning where the honest advice is not when to go but whether to. It is a convenience and it can be out of date — always check your own government's current travel advice before booking.
