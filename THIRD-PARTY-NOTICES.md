## Map data

`land.js` is generated by `tools/build_map.py` from the `world-atlas@2`
countries dataset delivered through jsDelivr. The geometry originates from
Natural Earth. Natural Earth states that all versions of its raster and vector
map data are in the public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The `world-atlas` npm package is distributed under the ISC licence. Its package
and source notices remain applicable to the downloaded TopoJSON representation.
